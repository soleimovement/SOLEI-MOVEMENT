Frequently Asked Questions

Where do sessions take place?

Private sessions are available throughout South Florida at your home, private studio, hotel, vacation rental, or another agreed-upon location. Travel availability and any applicable travel fees will be confirmed before booking.

What should I bring?

Simply wear comfortable clothing you can move in and bring water. Any equipment or mat requirements will be confirmed before your session.

Do I need Pilates experience?

Not at all. Every session is tailored to your experience level, whether you're completely new to Pilates or have years of practice.

How long is each session?

Most sessions are approximately 50–60 minutes, depending on the experience you book.

Can I book with a friend?

Absolutely. Partner sessions and small private groups are available for those who prefer to move together.

What is your cancellation policy?

To respect both your time and ours, cancellations or rescheduling requests must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Changes made within 24 hours may be subject to the full session fee.

How do I book?

If you're new to SOLEI, begin by booking your Intro Session above. Interested in a partner session or private group? Submit an inquiry, and we'll help create the experience that's right for you.

Do you provide mats and equipment?

Yes. Equipment needs vary depending on the session and location. We'll let you know exactly what is provided and if there's anything you'll need to have available beforehand.

What areas do you serve?

SOLEI currently serves Palm Beach County and surrounding South Florida areas. If you're planning a destination session or event, reach out—I'd love to discuss travel options.

What if I have injuries or physical limitations?

Every session is personalized to your body and your goals. Before your first session, we'll discuss any injuries, limitations, or concerns so your movement experience feels safe, supportive, and effective.
