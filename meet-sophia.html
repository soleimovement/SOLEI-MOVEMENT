Meet Sophia

Movement changed my life. Now, I create spaces where it can change the way others feel, too.

I'm Sophia, the founder of SOLEI—a movement and wellness brand rooted in confidence, connection, and meaningful community.

After teaching thousands of classes and working with people from all walks of life, I realized that people were looking for more than a workout. They wanted to feel stronger, supported, welcomed, and connected.

SOLEI was created to offer exactly that: intentional movement experiences that leave you feeling better than when you arrived.

Explore SOLEI

More Than Movement

For me, movement has never been only about what happens during the workout. It is about the confidence you carry afterward, the people you meet along the way, and the way an experience makes you feel.

That belief shapes every SOLEI session, event, and gathering.

How SOLEI Began

My journey into movement began with my own desire to feel stronger, more confident, and more connected to myself. Over time, teaching became more than something I did—it became one of the clearest expressions of who I am.

Through every class, I saw how movement could shift a person's energy, build confidence, and create real connection. I also saw how important it was for people—especially women who have not always felt represented in wellness spaces—to feel genuinely welcomed.

SOLEI grew from that vision: to create movement experiences that feel elevated, inclusive, intentional, and deeply human.

What I Believe

Strength Is More Than Physical

The way you move can shape how you carry yourself, trust yourself, and show up in the world.

Connection Matters

The most memorable wellness experiences are the ones that make people feel seen, supported, and included.

Details Create the Experience

The music, energy, setting, and feeling all matter. Thoughtfulness is what transforms a class into a moment.

The Vision Behind SOLEI

SOLEI is designed to live beyond a single class. It is a growing movement community where private coaching, celebrations, wellness gatherings, retreats, and partnerships can all exist under one shared purpose.

The vision is to continue creating experiences that help people slow down, reconnect, build confidence, and feel part of something meaningful.

Whether you join SOLEI for a private session, celebrate with your closest friends, attend a Signature Event, or partner with the brand, the intention remains the same: Move Strong. Feel Empowered.
